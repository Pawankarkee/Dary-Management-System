# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in our project a
respectful, inclusive and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism

Examples of unacceptable behavior by participants include:

- Harassment and discriminatory comments
- Publishing others' private information
- Insulting or demeaning comments

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported by contacting the project maintainers. All complaints will be reviewed
and investigated and will result in a response that is deemed necessary and
appropriate to the circumstances.
